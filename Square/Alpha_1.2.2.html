How to play

  • Click the green square (You) to open your move range.
  • Click inside the range to preview a move, then click again to confirm it.
  • If an enemy collides with you, the game ends.
  • Each move costs one of your Moves. When you run out, end your turn.
  • End Turn (button or Enter) advances time: enemies step toward you and new threats appear.
  • An X on the ground is a future enemy — it turns solid after a few turns, so plan around it.
  • Spend points in the Shop to boost income, range, move count, and more.
  • Shortcuts: Enter confirm/end turn, Esc close menus, H help.
  • Bosses appear every 25 turns;
  • Bosses: Evil Mage - faster enemy scaling
