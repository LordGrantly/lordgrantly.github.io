Enchanting Table

Enchant tools, weapons and armor from your inventory (unequip them first). Each enchant is a random pick from the list shown, and an item can hold up to 4 enchants. It uses up Mana Stones plus the materials the item was crafted from (not Sticks), and the cost triples with every enchant the item already has.

Arcane Table

Spend Mana Stones to unlock new spells and to upgrade the spells you know (up to level 8). Every upgrade costs 2 times more than the one before. You cast unlocked spells with a Spell Book as your weapon.
